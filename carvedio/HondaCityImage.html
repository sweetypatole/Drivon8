                      Collision Mitigating Braking System

                      Provides advance warning where risk of collision is imminent, through audio and visual alerts to the driver. Applies brakes to slow down the speed of the car and mitigates collision.

                      Road Departure Mitigation System

                      Provides advance warning through audio and visual alerts to the driver when the car seems to deviate from the lane. Provides steering wheel support when the car seems to deviate from the road boundaries.

                      Lane Keeping Assist System

                      Assists the driver to steer the wheel and helps the car to drive in the middle of the lane when cruising along the highway.

                      Adaptive Cruise Control

                      Assists acceleration and braking operation when cruising on highways while maintaining safe distance from preceding car.

                      Auto High-Beam

                      Switches between high beam and low beam automatically at night to support good visibility for driver as well as preceding or oncoming car.

                                  FAQs

                                  What is the mileage of Honda City?

                                  The mileage offered by 1.5L i-VTEC petrol engine in Honda City is 18.4 km/l* in the 7-Speed CVT and 17.77 km/l* in 6-speed MT. *T & C Apply

                                  What are the colours available in the Honda City?

                                  The Honda City is available in the following colours: Obsedian Blue, Radiant Red Metallic, Platinum White Pearl, Meteoroid Grey Metallic, Golden Brown Metallic, Lunar Silver Metallic

                                  What is the boot space of Honda City?

                                  The boot space of the Honda City is 506 litres.

                                  What are the safety features available in the Honda City?

                                  Here’s a list of the primary safety features available in the Honda City: 1st in Segment^ LanewatchTM Camera (Uniquely Mounted for Damage Protection) 1st in Segment^ 6 Airbags System (Dual Front I-SRS^ Airbags, Front Seats I-side Airbags & Side Curtain Airbags) 1st in Segment^ Agile Handling Assist Vehicle Stability Assist (VSA) Tyre Pressure Monitoring System (Deflation Warning System) 4 Rear Parking Sensors Emergency Stop Signal (ESS) Hill Start Assist (HSA) Multi-Angle Rear Camera Auto Headlamps with Auto-Off Timer, Follow-Me-Home & Lead-Me-To-Car Functions All Seats Head Restraints with 3-point ELR Seatbelts Auto Dimming Interior Mirror All Power Windows Auto up/down with Pinch Guard ABS with EBD ACETM Body Structure Variable Intermittent Wiper Rear ISOFIX Anchorages & Tether Immobilizer with Anti-Theft Alarm

                                  Is Honda City available in diesel version in India?

                                  No, Honda City is not available in diesel version.

                                  Is LPG/CNG versions available of Honda City in India?

                                  No, Honda City is not available in LPG/CNG versions.

                                  What is the transmission type of Honda City?

                                  1.5L DOHC i-VTEC in Honda City is available in 7-speed CVT and 6-speed MT.
