Battery Electric Vehicle (BEV)

Fully electric – runs only on a battery.

No petrol, no diesel, no engine oil.

You just charge and drive with zero emissions.

Examples: Tata Nexon EV, Tesla Model 3.

Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicle (PHEV)

You can charge it like an EV and also use fuel for longer trips.
Best for people who want EV benefits without range anxiety.

  • a petrol/diesel engine
  • an electric motor with battery
  • Better mileage
  • Example: MG Hector Plus Hybrid (India)

Hybrid Electric Vehicle (HEV)

Runs on fuel + electric motor, but cannot be charged using a plug.
Battery charges automatically using braking and the engine.

  • Strong build
  • Gives better mileage than normal cars.
  • Example: Toyota Hycross.

FCEV (Fuel Cell Electric Vehicle)

Runs on hydrogen fuel cells instead of a battery.
Releases only water vapor, making it very eco-friendly..

  • Powered by hydrogen, releases only water vapor.
  • Long range and quick refueling, but hydrogen stations are limited.
  • Example: Toyota Mirai
